  • Drafts a grade and feedback for each submission, following your rubric and your notes.

  • Writes replies to Inbox messages, student comments and discussions, and writes announcements.

The right feedback reaches the right student

  • When it grades, each AI request holds one student's one submission, with the name left out.
  • Before a grade posts, DoTask fetches that submission again and checks it is the same student and the same attempt.
  • Each student has their own color, and the button says their name.

For students

Open a page or a PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel file, and talk to your tutor. It explains, quizzes you and makes flashcards. Interrupt it any time. It can see your screen, which you can switch off, and it can scroll the page and go to the next page of a lesson with you.

For everything else on the web

Tell it what you need. It reads the page, scrolls, clicks and fills in forms. You choose how much freedom it gets: review every step, get asked only before anything that sends, buys or deletes, or let it run on its own. Stop it at any moment.

Install it in three minutes

  1. Unzip the download. Right-click the file and choose Extract All.
  2. Turn on Developer mode. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (edge://extensions in Edge) and switch on Developer mode, at the top right.
  3. Add DoTask. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder you unzipped.
  4. Connect the free AI. Press Alt+Shift+D to open DoTask, then go to Settings. Paste a free key from aistudio.google.com/apikey and press Test connection.

Your data

Student records, drafts and study files stay in your browser. There is no DoTask server. Your AI requests go straight to the AI service you pick.

On a free Google key, Google may use requests to improve its models. Submission text is part of those requests, so for real student work use a paid key, which costs a few cents for a whole class, or a local AI model such as Ollama.

What it will not do

It will not take graded quizzes or exams, click through courses to mark them complete, solve CAPTCHAs, get around site restrictions, or type passwords and payment details.
